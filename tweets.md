1.How do you discover sources in a web archive containing millions of sources? This article proposes AI-assisted document discovery as an approach. Here the research question of the historians leads the search for sources to close read.

2.From our own research field of childhood history we use a local LLM (GPT-OSS-120B) to sort and classify archived webpages from a specific domain into categories that can be used to answer our questions. 

3.Every categorization returned direct quotes from the document, the models reasoning and a URL to the original archived webpage, so that each match can be checked. Of 1,6 million quotes, 99,9% were found in the source. This method surfaced pages where children were visibly present, like an Internet Relay Chat, that browsing the archive would have missed. 

4.The authors argue that the method fits smaller curated web collections as it is heavy on compute.
